## About the course

This practical course shows participants how to use agentic AI throughout product development. Learners examine where autonomous agents add value, design coordinated workflows, improve agent performance, and evaluate deployed solutions with appropriate human oversight, security, and governance controls.

The course connects product research, requirements, prototyping, testing, launch, and continuous improvement through evidence-led agentic workflows.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, learners should be able to:

1. Evaluate agentic AI builders by comparing their strengths and limitations.
2. Apply optimisation techniques to improve agentic AI performance.
3. Evaluate the benefits and trade-offs of deploying agentic AI solutions.

## Topics covered

1. Evaluating agentic AI tools for product research and development
2. Building and optimising agentic AI product development workflows
3. Deploying and evaluating agentic AI-powered product solutions
