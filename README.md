## About the course

This course develops practical product-management capability for agentic AI. Learners evaluate builders on a common task, turn research evidence into product decisions, define bounded agent roles and handoffs, create evaluation sets, optimise a prototype, and recommend a governed deployment posture.

The remapped material follows the current course specification and expands the original courseware using the supplied *AI Product Management* and *Building AI-Powered Products* references. Slides stay conceptual and visual; detailed procedures, troubleshooting and checks are in the Learner Guide and activity folders.

## Learning outcomes

- Evaluate agentic AI builders for product research and development, comparing strengths, limitations, resources and skills.
- Apply optimisation techniques to improve the efficiency, quality and control of agentic product-development workflows.
- Evaluate the benefits and trade-offs of deploying agentic AI-powered product solutions and recommend a governed path forward.

## Topics covered

1. Evaluating Agentic AI Tools for Product Research and Development
2. Building and Optimising Agentic Product Development Workflows
3. Deploying and Evaluating Agentic AI-Powered Product Solutions
